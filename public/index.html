QUANTUM PULSE

phase-shift arena combat

An original game · v0.1.0 ·

Solo Survival

Survive escalating waves from the quantum rifts. Pick an upgrade between waves. A Singularity Titan emerges every fifth wave.

Online Free-for-All

Server-authoritative deathmatch for 2–12 runners. Leave the room code empty for quick match, or share a code with friends.

Co-op Rift Defense

Up to four runners defend the reactor in Reactor Null. Stay tethered for bonuses, revive downed allies with E, and trigger Pulse together for a Rift Surge.

Training Range

Offline sandbox with target dummies, every weapon, fast Pulse recharge and live movement telemetry. Press E to summon a practice swarm.

Settings

Click a binding, then press a key or mouse button. Esc cancels. Pause is always Esc.

How to Play

Movement

  • W A S D move · Mouse look
  • Space jump · jump again near a wall to wall-jump
  • Shift sprint · in the air: air dash
  • C/Ctrl slide · jump out of a slide to keep speed
  • Run along walls while holding W to wall-run
  • Q grapple: tap to attach (swing), hold to reel, Space to launch, Q again to release

Combat

  • LMB fire · RMB focus / deflect (Phase Blades)
  • 1–6 or wheel: weapons · R reload
  • F melee pulse · G gravity well · E interact / revive
  • X activate Phase Break at 100 Pulse Charge
  • Tab scoreboard · Esc pause

Quantum Pulse

Build Pulse Charge with skill: damage, kills, airborne kills, headshots, near-miss dodges, flying through quantum rings, sustained speed and kill combos. At 100, press X for Phase Break: 3 seconds in a parallel layer where enemy projectiles pass through you, other damage is reduced, and violet phase barriers become passable.

Gravity Fractures

Fractures pull, push or orbit everything nearby — you, enemies, projectiles and pickups. Use them to fling enemies into the void for environmental kills.

Momentum combat

Speed raises your damage (capped at +30%), landing hard creates a shockwave, slides stagger light enemies, and grapple launches empower your next shots.
