How to Play

Movement

  • W A S D move · Mouse look · you sprint automatically when running forward
  • Space jump · jump again near a wall to wall-jump
  • Space Space double-tap for a long jump: a forward burst in the air. One per jump, refilled when you land or wall-jump.
  • C slide while sprinting · jump out of a slide to keep your speed
  • Run along a wall while holding W to wall-run
  • Q grapple: tap to attach and swing, hold to reel in, Space to launch, Q again to let go

Aim and shoot

  • LMB fire · R reload · 1–6 or mouse wheel: switch weapon
  • Shift tap to zoom in, tap again to zoom out · or hold RMB to zoom while held
  • Zooming tightens your aim. The Vector Lance zooms into a sniper scope: hold LMB to charge, release to fire.
  • Aim for the head: headshots deal double damage and show HEADSHOT!
  • RMB with the Phase Blades deflects incoming bolts instead of zooming

Abilities

  • F melee pulse · G gravity well · E interact / revive
  • X activate Phase Break at 100 Pulse Charge
  • Tab scoreboard · Esc pause · every key can be changed in Settings → Controls

On a phone or tablet

  • Hold it sideways. Left thumb moves (push to the side to turn), drag on the right to look.
  • Fire shoots (drag on it to aim) · Aim toggles zoom · tap Jump twice for a long jump

Quantum Pulse

Build Pulse Charge with skill: damage, kills, airborne kills, headshots, near-miss dodges, flying through quantum rings, sustained speed and kill combos. At 100, press X for Phase Break: 3 seconds in a parallel layer where enemy projectiles pass through you, other damage is reduced, and blue hatched phase barriers become passable.

Gravity Fractures

Fractures pull, push or orbit everything nearby — you, enemies, projectiles and pickups. Use them to fling enemies into the void for environmental kills.

Momentum combat

Speed raises your damage (capped at +30%), landing hard creates a shockwave, slides stagger light enemies, and grapple launches empower your next shots.
